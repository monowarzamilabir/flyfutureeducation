Making global education accessible and honest

Fly Future Education was founded to close the gap between ambitious Bangladeshi students and the world's universities. Too many students lose time, money, and confidence to misinformation and unauthorized agents. We exist to be the opposite of that — a transparent, licensed consultancy that tells students the truth about their options, even when that means recommending a different country, program, or timeline than the one they first imagined.

Our counsellors handle university shortlisting, application filing, scholarship matching, visa documentation, and pre-departure preparation as one connected process, not a series of handoffs. We stay engaged with students well past their visa approval — through orientation, arrival, and their first weeks abroad.

We proudly support industry efforts — including recent initiatives by regulatory bodies such as FACD-CAB — to protect students from fraudulent consultancies and ensure only authorized agencies represent students' interests abroad.
